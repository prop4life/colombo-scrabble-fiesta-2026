What this Results Programme provides

The programme presents tournament information in a clear, mobile-friendly format and automatically builds detailed player analysis from published tournament results.

Pairings & StandingsPublished round-by-round for each tournament category.
Live Tournament DashboardCurrent tournament progress and leading players at a glance.
Featured MatchHighlights the Board 1 match for the latest published round.
Player ProfilesClickable individual profiles with detailed tournament statistics.
Average ScoresShows the player's average score and their opponents' average score.
Game HistoryA complete round-by-round record showing opponents, scores, results and spreads.
Head-to-Head StatisticsShows records, win percentage and spread against each opponent.
Winning Streak & Round LeaderAutomatically highlights current performance achievements.
Player PhotographsWhere a profile picture has been uploaded, it appears throughout supported player and match displays.
Mobile FriendlyDesigned for quick viewing during a live tournament on phones, tablets and computers.

Install Scrabble Fiesta on your Android phone

The results link can be installed on a mobile phone as a Progressive Web App (PWA). Once installed, it opens in an app-style window and provides convenient access to the tournament results.

Open the Colombo Scrabble Fiesta homepage
Colombo Scrabble Fiesta 2026 in Google Chrome.
Allow the page to finish loading completely.
Tap the ⋮ three-dot menu in the top-right corner of Chrome.
Look for Install app. Depending on your Chrome version, it may appear as Add to Home screen or Install and create shortcut.
Tap Install app. When Chrome asks “Install Scrabble Fiesta?”, tap Install.
Wait a few seconds. The Scrabble Fiesta icon should be added to your Home screen or app drawer.
Tap the icon. The results programme should open in its own app-style window.

Tip: Installing the app gives you a faster, more convenient way to return to the tournament results without repeatedly searching for the website.
